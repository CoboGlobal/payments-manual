This article describes the billing rules for Cobo Payments. Specific rates are subject to the sales contract you sign with Cobo.

## Fee Types

### Pay-In

Pay-in fees are subject to the sales contract.

### Payout

Payout fees are subject to the sales contract.

<Note>
Payouts involve on-chain operations that incur Gas Fees. These fees are fully covered by Cobo and require no additional payment from you.
</Note>

### Off-Ramp Fee

Off-Ramp fees are subject to the sales contract.

### Bridge Fee

Bridge refers to the transfer of assets from one blockchain network to another — for example, moving USDT from Ethereum to the Tron network. Bridge fees are subject to the sales contract.

## Fee Station and Monthly Minimum Spend

All fees incurred on Cobo are settled through **Fee Station**. The monthly minimum spend and offset rules are subject to the sales contract.

Fee Station uses a prepaid model — fees incurred during the month offset the monthly minimum spend. We recommend regularly checking your Fee Station balance to ensure sufficient funds and avoid disruption to payout operations. For top-up instructions, refer to [Top Up Fee Station](https://manuals.cobo.com/en/portal/fee-station/deposit).

## Overdue Accounts

If an account becomes overdue (unpaid invoices beyond a certain period), the system will freeze your team. During the freeze, payout operations will be unavailable. Please settle outstanding balances promptly to restore normal access.
